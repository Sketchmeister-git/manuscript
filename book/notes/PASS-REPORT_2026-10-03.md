## 1. What changed, and why

The modular edition was built so that any module could be read first. A book is read in order, and the order of the nine modules (A to I) was the order in which they were written, not an order that builds an argument. This pass turned the modules into a book that builds its argument cumulatively. It added the front and back matter needed for KDP, moved the working apparatus behind the Works Cited as the 26 September standing rulings require, and corrected six factual or quotation errors that had survived the modular edition.

## 2. The five-level edit

### Level 1: Sentences

- **Emphasis.** The modules bolded roughly one sentence in three, so nothing stood out. Bold now marks only definitions, run-in heads and propositions. Rhetorical emphasis is carried by sentence position and the occasional italic.
- **Self-reference.** All "this module" references now point to chapters. Phrases about the drafting process ("I wrote that as a disclaimer," "I did not know this literature," "I have not checked") were recast as claims about the subject, and the history they carried moved to Appendix A.
- **Hedged verbs kept.** Removing the inline grade tags (for example **[Supported]**) did not change any claim's strength. Where a tag had softened a claim, the verb now does that work ("the evidence supports," "is contested," "appears to"). The grades themselves are collected in Appendix C.
- **Citations.** Inline mentions such as "Douglas, Sutton and Cichocka" became APA 7 (Douglas et al., 2017). Classical works use original-and-translation dates (Nietzsche, 1883–1885/1909). Quotations carry section locators.
- **Agentless motives.** The book's own rule 2 bans sentences that give wants to something that cannot want. A sweep found none in the author's voice. "The algorithm wants engagement" appears only as an exhibit in Chapter 6.

### Level 2: Paragraphs

- **Premise restatements.** These were collapsed into short recalls with a chapter reference. Chapter 6's opening, which restated Module A's premise in full (about 450 words), is now a 150-word "situation, recalled." Chapter 7's restatement of the authorship finding and Chapter 4's restatement of Proposition 2 were shortened the same way.
- **Falsifier sections.** The "What would falsify this module" sections, roughly 2,600 words across nine modules, became one consolidated Appendix D. The one exception is Chapter 1's decisive experiment, which stays in the body because it sharpens Proposition 1.
- **"What this module does not claim" sections.** Their substance was folded into the paragraphs each limit qualifies. For example, "it does not make conspiracism harmless" now sits inside Chapter 6's licensing discussion. The standing limits (the book has the form of what it diagnoses; the support is selected) moved to Appendix D.
- **The Jung quotation audit.** In Module H it ran to about 900 words. It is now about 350 words in Chapter 8, told as a demonstration of an availability cascade, with the full trace in Appendix E.

### Level 3: Paragraph order within chapters

- **Chapter 1.** The H1–H4 rivals and their prediction table now come *before* the inventory, so the reader knows which row will decide the matter before the evidence arrives.
- **Chapter 2.** It now ends with a new section, *A lion with no dragon*, which carries the anonymous-authority premise (Proposition 4, with Fromm and Tocqueville) moved from Module C.
- **Chapter 5.** The Libet material now follows the positive evidence. It is presented as an item the reader will expect and that the book rests nothing on, not as part of the argument.
- **Chapter 6.** The objection that the form is merely copied, which Module C left unanswered, now gets a reply in place: copying explains how the form travels, while function explains why it is taken up. **This reply is new and untested; see §4.**

### Level 4: Chapter order within parts

| Part | Chapters (module) | Reason for the order |
|---|---|---|
| I The Condition | 1 (A) → 2 (B) → 3 (D) | B and D are one structure in two registers (the index's own pairing). D's verdict, "a nation of lions," depends on B. |
| II The Invisible Mechanism | 4 (E) → 5 (F) | E names the mechanism; F explains why it goes unseen. |
| III Two Counterfeits | 6 (C) → 7 (G) | C and G are the symmetry pair and are now adjacent. Both depend on F's authorship finding, which in the modular order came after C. |
| IV Construction | 8 (H) → 9 (I) | H names the capacity; I names the arrangements that build it. |

**The one dependency the move broke** was that F (now Chapter 5) uses "anonymous authority" before C (now Chapter 6) defines it. It was repaired by relocating Proposition 4 to the end of Chapter 2 (Level 3 above).

### Level 5: Arrangement of sections

- **Front matter:** half-title, title page, copyright page, epigraph (Nietzsche, Common translation, verified), contents, A Message from the Author, Preface.
- **Main matter:** Introduction (with the four-voice frame from *The Path to Renewal* and the five rules the book keeps), four parts, then a Conclusion that redraws the arc.
- **Back matter:** The Argument in Brief (abstract plus keywords by field), Glossary, Works Cited, Appendices A–E, Index.
- **Recommendation on the end material you asked about.** All three earn a place, for different readers. The **Glossary** matters most for this book. It is written as a translation table: each term gets its home field and the nearest equivalents in other fields, which directly addresses the cross-talk problem you described. The **Index** serves re-readers and scholars, and its entries mix thinkers with constructs so a psychologist and a philosopher land on the same pages. The **Abstract** is placed first in the back matter as *The Argument in Brief*, and it doubles as the source text for the KDP book description and for library or ResearchGate listings.

## 3. Integration of *The Path to Renewal*

The essay's four-stage arc (Jaynes → Nietzsche → Watts → Wallace) became the book's frame, in the Introduction and the Conclusion. That is where it is strongest: as a map that is wrong in an instructive place.

- **Kept:** Jaynes as a *parable* of overt authority. His history is contested, and the book says so and rests nothing on it. Nietzsche's death of the guarantee is kept as is.
- **Re-weighted:** Watts. The essay's stage III (ego dissolution as cosmic unity) does not survive the book's own Proposition 12, because a state that trying cannot reach is a poor fourth-stage goal. What survives is the law of reversed effort, used as a design constraint.
- **Redrawn:** Wallace. The essay ends in "a bottom-up transformation of consciousness" through chosen attention. Chapters 5, 7 and 9 contradict that ending: awareness is not the mechanism, assent discharges, and instructions addressed to states fail. The Conclusion keeps *This Is Water*'s diagnosis, treats its reception as a case of ambient critique, and replaces the essay's fourth stage with two halves (compulsory process plus norms). The Conclusion closes with a new claim answering an objection the modular edition never raised: a norm is anonymous authority whose authors are everyone in the room.
- **Not carried forward:** the essay's reference list, which is mostly Facebook, Reddit, YouTube, Quora and Medium pages, and its errors ("New Senserity"; a "single-entendre" principle attributed to Samuel Johnson). Its four thinkers are cited from their own books.

## 4. Adversarial pass: weaknesses recorded here, not in the book

These are logged for the next pass. Under the standing rulings, the book states its claims plainly and only Appendix D records limits.

1. **The weakest argument** is still the national frame (Chapters 2 and 3). The comparative test (a positive-freedom polity under the same conditions) has not been run, and the book's distinctive American reading depends on it. *Strengthening route:* comparative trust and participation data (for example European Social Survey and World Values Survey items on efficacy) for one or two social-democratic polities. That is a desk study, not new data collection.
2. **The heaviest unearned assumption** is Proposition 10's identity claim (interval tolerance = incompetence tolerance). It is argued from convergence, which by the book's own rule 3 is not corroboration. *Strengthening route:* the dissociation literature on distress tolerance versus frustration tolerance in clinical psychology may already settle it. A construct-overlap audit is the next step.
3. **The new reply to the copying objection (Chapter 6)** asserts selection without evidence. It reads well and is untested. *Option:* keep it but grade it **Speculative** in Appendix C (not yet done; author decision 6), or cut it back to the modular edition's open concession.
4. **Where the framing tilts toward its own conclusion:** Chapter 7's datum ("assent has changed almost nothing") is asserted without a consumption or trust time series. The book needs one citable series. Pew or Gallup on institutional confidence would cover Chapter 3 as well.
5. **What a hostile, competent reader attacks first:** the claim that civil conflict is the one threat with real individual leverage (Chapter 9's "load-bearing conclusion"). Polarisation research (affective polarisation, elite cueing) would argue that conduct norms among ordinary people barely move the aggregate. The book currently has no citation either way.
6. **Selection in the evidence base.** The disconfirming literature on choice blindness (for example the debate over detection rates in high-stakes choices) and on moral-distress scale validity is not cited.
