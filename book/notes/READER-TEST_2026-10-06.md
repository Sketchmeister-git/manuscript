## Not changed, and why
- **"Last Man is not a catastrophe" vs "the lion stage can end in the Last Man" (4).** These are consistent (a stable equilibrium versus a possible terminus), and the text says both. Left as is.
- **H3 "substantially correct" but not the explanation (1).** Deliberate; the chapter says exactly where H3 fails.
- **Whether the lion-to-efficacy link is argued or asserted (9).** The reader is right that it rests on Fromm's formal structure and on analogy. The book states the analogy and now says what would show it wrong, but it does not add an argument. Left for the author; see the next-pass queue.
- **Scope (US vs "wealthy democracy") (1).** Preface now says the frame is American and points to the comparative test.
